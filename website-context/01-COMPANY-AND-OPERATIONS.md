# 01 — Company Profile, Operations & Business Model

## 1. Company Identity

- **Brand Name:** Genomed Pharmaceuticals (Short name: *Genomed*)
- **Tagline:** *"Your health, our priority"*
- **Core Positioning:** An evidence-backed herbal and Ayurvedic medicine manufacturer combining classical Ayurvedic formulations with contemporary pharmaceutical-grade manufacturing rigor.
- **Corporate Mission:**
  - Produce consistent, batch-traceable herbal medicines where every batch adheres to strict master formulas.
  - Source raw botanicals and excipients with verified identity and organoleptic purity.
  - Maintain documentation that withstands statutory audits, inquiries, and quality inspections.
  - Deliver honest and accessible pricing for patients across India.
  - Provide predictable, forecast-driven supply chains for regional stockists and distributor networks.
- **Core Values:**
  1. *Do it properly or not at all:* If a batch misses its release specifications, it does not ship.
  2. *Respect the tradition:* Modernizing control and documentation without diluting authentic Ayurvedic principles.
  3. *Write it down:* Every formula, parameter, deviation, and corrective action is recorded.
  4. *Be easy to work with:* Direct communication, fixed trade margins, honest lead times, and accessible support.

## 3. Regulatory & Quality Framework

- **Drug Manufacturing Licence:**  
  Issued by the State Licensing Authority, Department of AYUSH, Government of Uttar Pradesh, under the *Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940*.
- **Good Manufacturing Practice (GMP):**  
  Facility layout, airflow, hygiene, machinery, and quality control benches adhere to **Schedule T of the Drugs and Cosmetics Rules, 1945** (GMP for Ayurvedic, Siddha, and Unani medicines).
- **In-House Quality Control Laboratory:**  
  Conducts wet chemical analysis, organoleptic inspections, bulk density, pH, fill-volume verification, and finished-lot release testing prior to commercial release.
- **Traceability Guarantee:**  
  Every carton and container features indelible batch numbering, manufacturing date, and expiration date tied to retained plant lot records.

## 4. Commercial Verticals & Partnership Models

Genomed works with commercial partners via three primary pathways:

### A. Territory Distributors & Stockists
- **Model:** Exclusive or non-exclusive geographic territory appointments.
- **Target Partners:** Pharmaceutical distribution houses, wholesale stockists, and retail distributors supplying medical stores and Ayurvedic dispensaries.
- **Partner Commitments:**
  - Clear territory allocation formalized in writing before initial dispatch.
  - Locked trade margins and credit windows (never re-bargained order-by-order).
  - Batch certificates of analysis (CoA) accompanying consignments.
  - Production buffer matched to distributor monthly sales forecasts to avert stock-outs.
  - Field marketing collateral, visual aids, and point-of-sale literature provided.

### B. Institutional Supply
- **Model:** Bulk institutional contracts.
- **Target Partners:** Ayurvedic colleges, multi-specialty hospitals, private nursing homes, government dispensaries, charitable trusts, and community clinics.
- **Offerings:** Direct supply, statutory vendor qualification packages, batch-specific analytical paperwork, and structured consignment schedules.

### C. Third-Party Contract Manufacturing
- **Model:** Contract manufacturing / Private label partnerships.
- **Offerings:** Formulating, manufacturing, filling, and packaging Ayurvedic products under partner client brands using Genomed's existing master formulas or client-specified proprietary compositions (subject to technical feasibility and licensing approval).

## 5. 4-Stage Distributor Onboarding Workflow

1. **Brief Submission:** Partner provides targeted geographic territory, active therapeutic domains, existing distribution lines, and monthly sales forecast.
2. **Proposal Turnaround (Within 2 Business Days):** Genomed replies with full product literature, dealer pricing matrix, trade margins, and territory MOQ (Minimum Order Quantities).
3. **Formalized Terms:** Written agreement establishing territory perimeter, credit framework, dispatch cycles, and promotional assistance.
4. **Initial Despatch:** Opening commercial stock, analytical documents (CoAs), and promotional visual aids are dispatched simultaneously.
