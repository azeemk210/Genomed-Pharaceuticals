## Philosophy & Vision
- **Core Philosophy:** *"A release test tells you what happened. It does not make a good batch. Everything upstream of it — the material we accept, the parameters we hold, the deviations we close — is where quality is actually decided."*
- **Our Quality Vision:** A standard that does not move. Operators are trained against the written process and encouraged to stop a run if a departure occurs.
- **Quality Management System:** One unified system across formulation development, production, quality control, and despatch.

---

## The 5 Working Practices
1. **One standard, every batch:** Master formula, equipment train, and process parameters are fixed in writing; changes require documented change control.
2. **Material in before product out:** Raw herbs, excipients, and packaging are quarantined and tested prior to batch issue.
3. **Deviations are recorded, not absorbed:** Departures are investigated for root cause and closed with corrective action before release.
4. **Lessons travel across products:** Findings on one formulation are systematically reviewed across all products sharing materials or equipment.
5. **Retention and traceability:** Retained samples and batch manufacturing records (BMRs) held for every lot manufactured.

---

## "From Herb to Pack" — 8-Stage Pipeline
1. **Intake:** Herbs, excipients, and packaging received against specification, sampled, and placed in quarantine.
2. **Identification:** Identity, purity, and organoleptic checks carried out prior to production release.
3. **Preparation:** Extraction, decoction, and preparation of the herbal base carried out to master parameters.
4. **Formulation:** Blending and compounding against the written formula with quantities verified and witnessed.
5. **In-process checks:** Sampling at defined intervals (appearance, consistency, physical parameters).
6. **Filling:** Filling, sealing, labelling, fill-volume verification, and inline batch coding.
7. **Release testing:** Finished-product testing against release specifications; no batch leaves quarantine until analytical clearance is on file.
8. **Documentation:** Batch records completed, retained samples archived, and commercial lot released for dispatch.

## Regulatory Compliance
- **Drug Manufacturing Licence:** Issued by the Government of Uttar Pradesh under the Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940.
- **Schedule T GMP:** Compliance with the Good Manufacturing Practice rules for ASU drugs under Drugs and Cosmetics Rules, 1945.
