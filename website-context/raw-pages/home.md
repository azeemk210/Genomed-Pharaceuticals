## "Who We Are" Process
- **01 Purity of input:** Herbs and excipients are identified, sampled and tested on arrival. Material that fails identity or purity checks is quarantined and returned — it never reaches a batch.
- **02 Process discipline:** Each formulation follows a written master formula with defined parameters. Deviations are recorded, investigated and closed before the batch is released.
- **03 Evidence on every pack:** Batch number, manufacturing date and expiry are printed on every unit and tied to retained samples and release records held at the plant.
- **04 Dependable supply:** Production planned against distributor forecasts so stockists are not left short of a fast-moving line at the point they need it.

## Quality & Manufacturing Credentials
- **Drug Manufacturing Licence:** Manufactured under licence issued by the Government of Uttar Pradesh, under the Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940.
- **Schedule T — Good Manufacturing Practice:** Facility, equipment and process controls maintained to Schedule T of the Drugs and Cosmetics Rules, 1945.
- **In-house Quality Control Laboratory:** Raw-material identification, in-process checks and finished-product release testing.
- **Batch Traceability:** Every pack carries a batch number, manufacturing date and expiry traceable to its raw-material records.
