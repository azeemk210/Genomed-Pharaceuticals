# 02 — Product Catalog, Formulations & Therapeutic Areas

## 1. Portfolio Philosophy

Genomed adheres to a curated catalog strategy:
> *"Ten formulations across eight therapeutic areas. A tight range we can make consistently beats a sprawling catalogue we can only make occasionally."*

Every formulation uses **100% herbal ingredients**, produced across **4 dosage presentations** (Syrup, Oral Liquid, Reconstitutable Powder, Hard Gelatin / Vegetarian Capsule). Compositions and exact excipient matrices are held in confidential master formulas and released to qualified medical/distribution practitioners via requested technical literature.
